RECYCLING PROCESS
📦

Segregation / Sorting

Segregation or sorting involves separating waste into categories for efficient recycling, disposal, or reuse.

✔

Washing

Washing removes contaminants from recyclables, ensuring clean materials for processing into new products or reuse.

📦

Baling

Baling compresses recyclable materials like plastics, paper, or metals into compact blocks for easier transport and storage.

⬤

Pelletization

Pelletization converts shredded plastic waste into uniform pellets through melting and extrusion, enabling easier recycling and reuse.
